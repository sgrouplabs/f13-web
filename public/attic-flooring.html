Professional Attic Flooring That Lasts

Your attic has usable square footage that's going to waste. Whether you're storing seasonal decorations, tools, or everyday household items, a properly installed attic floor makes all the difference. F13 LLC installs plywood subfloors, attic decking panels, and full raised floor systems throughout Greater Houston — Katy, Cypress, Spring, and surrounding areas.

We work with your existing attic structure and insulation. Our crews evaluate the joists, check insulation depth, and recommend the right plywood thickness and panel spacing for your specific load needs — whether you're storing lightweight bins or heavier equipment. Every installation includes a free in-person attic evaluation before we start.

Plywood Attic Floor Installation

We install plywood subfloors using the correct thickness for your joist spacing and load requirements. Proper plywood for attic floor installation means a surface that won't flex, sag, or compromise your insulation.

Attic Decking Panels

Attic decking panels offer a clean, consistent surface with better load distribution than random plywood sheets. Ideal for creating organized storage zones over insulation with minimal interference to airflow.

Raised Floor Systems

A raised attic floor keeps plywood above insulation for proper thermal performance while still creating solid, walkable storage. We use adjustable or fixed-height systems depending on your insulation depth.

Attic Subfloor & Flooring Over Insulation

Installing flooring over attic insulation requires the right technique to avoid compressing insulation and reducing R-value. We ensure proper spacing and support so your insulation keeps working and your floor stays solid.
