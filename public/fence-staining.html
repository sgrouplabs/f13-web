Professional Fence Staining That Protects Your Investment

Houston's humidity, intense sun, and heavy rain are tough on wood fences. Without proper staining and sealing, wood fences in our area can begin to gray, crack, and deteriorate within a single season. That's why F13 LLC offers professional fence staining throughout Houston and the surrounding areas — we handle the complete process from deep power washing through final stain application.

Our fence staining service includes a thorough cleaning to remove mold, mildew, and weathering, followed by professional-grade stain application that penetrates and seals the wood. Whether you have a privacy fence, picket fence, or ranch rail, we match the right stain product to your wood type and your aesthetic preferences. We also work with HOA guidelines to ensure your fence meets community standards.

Fence Power Washing

Deep cleaning removes mold, mildew, algae, and accumulated grime before any stain goes on. A properly cleaned surface ensures the stain adheres correctly and lasts longer.

Fence Staining

We apply professional-grade wood stain in opaque, semi-transparent, or natural finishes. Stain protects against UV damage, moisture, and insect damage while enhancing the natural beauty of your wood fence.

Fence Sealing

Sealing locks in the stain and provides an additional barrier against Houston's harsh weather. A sealed fence resists moisture penetration, reduces warping, and maintains its color longer between re-staining cycles.

Should You Stain Both Sides of a Fence?

The short answer: yes, staining both sides of your fence is the best practice when possible. Fences face weathering from both sides — Houston humidity and rain drive moisture into the wood from either direction. Staining only one side leaves the other vulnerable to faster deterioration.

That said, if your fence shares a property line with a neighbor, staining your side is the standard and is what most HOA guidelines require. We can stain one side or both depending on your situation and preference. We'll discuss the options at your free estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does fence staining last in Houston?
In Houston's climate, a professional fence staining job typically lasts 2–3 years before you'll start noticing fading or weathering. A sealed fence may go 3–4 years. We use commercial-grade stains that outperform the DIY products you'd find at a home improvement store.
Do I need to power wash my fence before staining?
Yes — power washing is essential for a long-lasting stain job. Without it, the stain adheres to the dirt layer rather than the wood fiber and will peel or fail within months. Our fence power washing service prepares the surface properly so the stain bonds correctly.
What stain color should I choose for my fence?
That depends on your preference and HOA requirements if applicable. We offer a range of transparent, semi-transparent, and solid-color stains in natural wood tones and neutrals. During your estimate we can show you stain samples on your actual fence wood so you can see exactly how each option looks before committing.
How much does fence staining cost in Houston?
Fence staining cost depends on fence height, linear footage, wood condition, and whether power washing is included. We provide a free in-person estimate so you know the exact cost upfront — no surprises.
Can you stain a fence that has already been stained?
Yes, in most cases. If the old stain is still intact and the wood underneath is in good condition, we can apply a new coat after proper cleaning. If the previous stain is peeling heavily, we'll need to strip it before re-staining. We assess this during the estimate.
