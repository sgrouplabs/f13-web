Frequently Asked Questions

How long does concrete take to cure in Houston?
Concrete reaches functional strength in about 7 days in Houston's warm climate, but full cure takes 28 days. You can typically walk on it lightly after 24–48 hours and drive on it after 7–10 days. We'll give you specific guidance based on the mix we use and the weather at time of pour.
Do I need a permit for a concrete slab in Houston?
For most residential concrete slabs in Greater Houston — walkways, patios, AC pads, trash can slabs — a permit is not typically required since they're not structural and are under a certain square footage. Driveway extensions may have different requirements depending on your city and HOA. We know the local codes and will advise you during the estimate.
What psi concrete do you use?
We use a minimum 4,000 psi mix for all our concrete work, which is more than adequate for residential slabs, walkways, and pads. For heavier load applications like driveway extensions, we adjust the mix design and reinforcement accordingly. Every project is sized to the load it's expected to bear.
How much does a concrete slab cost in Houston?
Concrete cost depends on square footage, thickness, reinforcement needs, site access, and finish type. We provide a free in-person estimate so you know exactly what your specific project will cost before we start.
Will my concrete crack in Houston's heat?
Some hairline cracking is normal and expected in concrete — it doesn't affect structural integrity. We reduce cracking by installing control joints at the correct spacing, using proper sub-base preparation, and using the right concrete mix for Houston's clay soil conditions. A properly installed concrete slab with control joints will crack in the joints where it's designed to, not randomly across the surface.
