Our Fence Staining Process in Katy

Every fence we stain follows a repeatable four-step process that maximizes adhesion, color uniformity, and longevity. Skipping any step is the reason most DIY and budget stain jobs fail within a year.

1

Inspection & Repair

We walk the entire fence line before any work begins. Loose pickets get re-fastened, rotted sections get replaced, and protruding nail heads get hammered flush. Staining over damaged boards traps moisture behind the finish, so we fix the structure before we touch the stain.

2

Power Washing & Cleaning

We use a commercial-grade power washer with a wood-safe tip and a biodegradable cleaning solution to strip away mildew, dirt, pollen, and the fuzzy gray layer of degraded wood fiber that forms on untreated fences. This step opens the wood pores so the stain can penetrate deep rather than sit on the surface.

3

Drying & Moisture Check

This is the step most contractors rush. After washing, the wood needs time to dry to an ideal moisture content — typically 24 to 48 hours depending on humidity and sun exposure. We use a moisture meter to confirm the wood is ready before we apply a single drop of stain. Staining wet wood traps moisture and guarantees peeling.

4

Stain Application

We apply exterior-grade oil-based or water-based stain using sprayer and back-brush technique — spraying for speed and brushing for penetration. Two coats on raw wood, one coat on previously stained wood in good condition. Every edge, gap, and fence-top gets coverage because those are the spots where moisture gets in first.

Fence Staining FAQs — Katy, TX

How long does fence staining last in Katy, TX?

In the Houston climate, a quality stain applied to properly prepped wood typically holds its color and protection for 3 to 5 years. Houston humidity and UV exposure are the main drivers — that's why we only use exterior-grade stains rated for Gulf Coast weather and re-application schedules that fit Katy conditions.

Do you pressure wash before staining?

Yes — surface prep is the difference between stain that lasts and stain that peels. Every fence we stain in Katy gets cleaned first to remove mildew, dirt, and grayed wood fibers, then allowed to dry to the correct moisture level before we apply any stain.

Is staining better than painting a fence?

For wood fences, staining wins almost every time. Stain soaks into the wood instead of sitting on top like paint, so it won't chip, peel, or blister. It moves with the wood as it expands and contracts through Houston's humidity swings, and touch-ups are far easier.

How much does fence staining cost in Katy?

Pricing depends on fence height, condition, and whether it needs cleaning or repairs first. We give free in-person quotes throughout Katy and the surrounding 77494, 77493, 77450 zip codes — we walk the fence line, measure, and give you an honest, no-pressure price.

When is the best time of year to stain a fence in Katy?

Spring and fall are ideal — temperatures between 60 and 85 degrees with low humidity and no rain in the forecast. Summer heat can cause stain to dry too fast, and winter moisture can slow curing. We monitor weather closely and schedule around ideal conditions for every job.
