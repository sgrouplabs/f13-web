Cypress has experienced explosive growth over the past decade, and with that growth came miles of new wood fencing in master-planned communities like Bridgeland, Towne Lake, and Fairfield. Our Fence Staining Process in Cypress

We follow the same thorough four-step process on every fence we stain. The goal is simple: make the finish last as long as possible by never skipping the prep work that cheap stain jobs ignore.

1

Inspection & Repair

We inspect every board, rail, and post along your fence line. Loose nails get driven or replaced, damaged pickets get swapped, and gate hardware gets checked for alignment. A fence needs to be structurally sound before any stain goes on — otherwise moisture seeps into the gaps and causes premature failure.

2

Power Washing & Cleaning

We use a commercial-grade pressure washer set to the correct PSI for wood — too much pressure damages the surface, too little leaves dirt behind. Combined with a wood-safe cleaning solution, this step strips away mildew, algae, pollen buildup, and the weathered gray layer that forms on exposed wood. Clean wood accepts stain evenly and deeply.

3

Drying & Moisture Check

After washing, the wood needs adequate drying time — usually 24 to 48 hours depending on Cypress's weather patterns. We test moisture content with a digital meter before proceeding. Staining wood that is still wet from washing traps moisture under the finish, causing bubbles, peeling, and mildew growth within months.

4

Stain Application

We spray the stain onto the fence using professional airless equipment, then immediately back-brush it into the wood grain. This two-step method ensures full penetration into the end grain, gaps, and rough spots where moisture attacks first. We apply two coats on raw wood and one coat on previously stained surfaces in good condition, taking care to protect landscaping and hardscaping throughout.

Fence Staining FAQs — Cypress, TX

How long does fence staining last in Cypress, TX?

In the Houston climate, a quality stain applied to properly prepped wood typically holds its color and protection for 3 to 5 years. Houston humidity and UV exposure are the main drivers — that's why we only use exterior-grade stains rated for Gulf Coast weather and re-application schedules that fit Cypress conditions.

Do you pressure wash before staining?

Yes — surface prep is the difference between stain that lasts and stain that peels. Every fence we stain in Cypress gets cleaned first to remove mildew, dirt, and grayed wood fibers, then allowed to dry to the correct moisture level before we apply any stain.

Is staining better than painting a fence?

For wood fences, staining wins almost every time. Stain soaks into the wood instead of sitting on top like paint, so it won't chip, peel, or blister. It moves with the wood as it expands and contracts through Houston's humidity swings, and touch-ups are far easier.

How much does fence staining cost in Cypress?

Pricing depends on fence height, condition, and whether it needs cleaning or repairs first. We give free in-person quotes throughout Cypress and the surrounding 77433, 77429 zip codes — we walk the fence line, measure, and give you an honest, no-pressure price.

When is the best time of year to stain a fence in Cypress?

Spring and fall months are ideal — temperatures between 60 and 85 degrees with low humidity and no rain in the forecast. We monitor Cypress weather closely and schedule around ideal conditions for every job. Summer heat can cause stain to dry too quickly, and winter moisture can extend curing time.
