Trash Can Pad FAQs

What size concrete pad do I need for my trash cans?

A standard 2-can residential setup requires a 4ft x 6ft pad. For 3 rolling bins (trash, recycling, yard waste), a 4ft x 8ft pad provides ample space without crowding property lines or gates.

How thick should a residential trash can concrete pad be?

We pour a solid 4-inch slab of 3,500 PSI concrete reinforced with rebar or fiber mesh over a compacted gravel sub-base to prevent cracking on Houston's shifting clay soils.

Do you handle HOA approval requirements?

Yes, our concrete pads are designed to meet typical Texas HOA guidelines, ensuring clean containment behind fence lines or along side-yard walkways.

How long does the concrete take to cure?

The pad is walk-ready in 24-48 hours. We recommend waiting 7 days before setting heavy bins on it for full surface strength, though it keeps curing to design strength over 28 days.

Will the pad drain properly and not pool water?

Every pad is poured with a slight drainage slope (about 1/8 inch per foot) away from your home and fence line, so rain runs off instead of pooling under your bins.
