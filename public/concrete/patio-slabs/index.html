Patio Slab FAQs

How thick should a concrete patio slab be?

We pour a minimum 4-inch thick slab of 4,000 PSI concrete for standard residential patios. For hot tubs or heavy structures, we increase the thickness to 5-6 inches with additional rebar reinforcement.

What finish options are available for concrete patios?

We offer broom-finish (standard brushed non-slip), exposed aggregate (pebble-top), and stamped concrete patterns. Each has different maintenance requirements and price points.

How long does it take to pour a concrete patio?

Most standard patio slabs are poured and finished in 1-2 days. Cure time before furniture goes on is 7 days. Full structural cure takes 28 days.

Does a concrete patio need to be sealed?

We recommend sealing concrete patios in Houston's climate to protect against moisture, staining, and UV fading. Sealing extends the life of the finish and makes cleaning easier.

Will my concrete patio crack in Texas heat?

Properly installed concrete with control joints, reinforced sub-base, and the right PSI mix will crack along control joints rather than randomly. Some hairline cracking is normal, but proper installation minimizes it significantly.
