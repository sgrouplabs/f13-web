Patio Slab FAQs — Cypress, TX

How much does a concrete patio slab cost in Cypress?

A standard 10x12 broom-finish patio slab typically costs between $1,200 and $2,400 depending on site access, base preparation needs, and finish. Exposed aggregate and stamped finishes add to the cost. Every figure is an estimate — we provide free in-person quotes.

How long does a patio take to pour in Cypress?

Most Cypress patio slabs are poured in 1-2 days. We can typically schedule within a week across Bridgeland, Towne Lake, Fairfield, and Coles Crossing.

What finish do you recommend for Cypress patios?

Broom-finish is the most durable and budget-friendly option. Exposed aggregate offers a decorative look with strong traction. Stamped concrete gives you a stone or paver appearance at a higher price point.

How long does concrete take to cure in Cypress weather?

In Cypress's warm climate, concrete reaches walkable strength in 24-48 hours. Furniture can go on after 7 days, and full structural cure is 28 days. We give specific guidance based on weather at pour time.
