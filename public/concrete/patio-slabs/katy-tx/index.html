Concrete Patios Built for Katy Backyards

Katy's master-planned communities have backyards designed for outdoor living — but many of them lack a proper concrete patio slab to anchor the space. A custom concrete patio transforms a bare yard into a usable outdoor room for grilling, dining with family, relaxing in the evening, or hosting neighborhood get-togethers.

F13 LLC pours residential concrete patio slabs throughout Katy neighborhoods including Cinco Ranch, Grand Lakes, Firethorne, Cane Island, Elyson, and everywhere in the 77494, 77493, and 77450 zip codes. We handle the forming, pouring, and finishing of 4-inch 4,000 PSI reinforced slabs in a single pour session, with broom, exposed aggregate, or stamped finishes.

Every Katy patio slab starts with proper site prep: we excavate to the right depth, lay and compact a gravel sub-base for drainage, install fiber mesh or rebar reinforcement, and set forms to your exact dimensions. We pitch the slab slightly away from your home so rain runs off instead of pooling against the foundation. Control joints are saw-cut at the right spacing to manage cracking, and we broom-finish for non-slip safety or apply your chosen decorative finish.

Most Katy patio installations are completed in 1-2 days depending on size and finish complexity. We price honestly up front with a free in-person estimate — no hidden fees, no minimum square footage requirements.

Patio Slab FAQs — Katy, TX

How much does a concrete patio slab cost in Katy?

A standard 10x12 broom-finish patio slab typically costs between $1,200 and $2,200 depending on site access, base preparation needs, and finish. Exposed aggregate and stamped finishes add to the cost. Every figure is an estimate — we provide free in-person quotes.

How long does a patio take to pour in Katy?

Most Katy patio slabs are poured in 1-2 days. We can typically schedule within a week across Cinco Ranch, Grand Lakes, Firethorne, Cane Island, and Elyson.

What finish do you recommend for Katy patios?

Broom-finish is the most durable and budget-friendly option for Katy's climate. Exposed aggregate offers a decorative look with strong traction. Stamped concrete gives you a stone or tile appearance at a higher price point.

Do Katy HOAs restrict patio slab finishes?

Some Katy area HOAs have guidelines for concrete finishes and placement. We can advise you based on your neighborhood's requirements during the free estimate.
