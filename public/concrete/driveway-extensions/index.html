Driveway Extension Specifications

5-Inch 4,000+ PSI

Driveway extensions get a thicker pour than patios or walkways — a minimum 5-inch slab with 4,000+ PSI concrete engineered to support vehicle loads without settling.

Rebar Reinforcement

Steel rebar reinforcement grid throughout the slab provides the tensile strength needed to handle daily vehicle traffic without cracking.

Base Prep & Drainage

Compacted gravel sub-base with proper grading so water runs off the extension rather than pooling — critical for Houston's heavy rain events and clay soil.

Finish Match

We broom-finish to match your existing driveway texture as closely as possible, with an expansion joint at the seam so the two slabs move independently.

Driveway Extension FAQs

Can you match an existing driveway's color and finish?

We do our best to match the existing color, texture, and finish of your current driveway. While a perfect match is never guaranteed with concrete due to natural curing variations, we get close enough that the joint line is the only visible seam.

How thick does a driveway extension need to be?

We pour a minimum 5-inch thick slab of 4,000+ PSI concrete for all driveway extensions, with rebar reinforcement. This is thicker than standard patio or walkway slabs because driveways carry the weight of vehicles.

Do I need a permit for a driveway extension in Houston?

Driveway extensions may require permits depending on your city, HOA, and the size of the addition. We know the local codes across Greater Houston and will advise you during the free estimate what permits apply.

How long does a driveway extension take to complete?

Most driveway extensions are formed, poured, and finished in 1-2 days. The concrete needs 7 days before you can park on it, and 28 days to reach full structural cure.

Will my driveway extension crack where it meets the old concrete?

We install a control joint or expansion material where the new slab meets your existing driveway. This allows the two slabs to move independently with temperature changes and soil shifts, preventing random cracking.
