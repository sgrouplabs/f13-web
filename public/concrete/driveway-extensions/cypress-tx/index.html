Driveway Additions Built for Cypress Homes

Cypress master-planned communities like Bridgeland, Towne Lake, and Fairfield are designed with clean curb appeal — but many of their homes were built with driveways that barely fit one car. If you have a second vehicle, a boat, or an RV, parking on the street is the only option without a driveway extension. A concrete extension solves this without the cost and disruption of tearing out and replacing the entire driveway.

F13 LLC specializes in driveway extensions across Cypress: widening an existing driveway by adding 3-4 feet, pouring a dedicated second car bay parallel to your current slab, or installing a turnaround pad at the end of a long driveway so you can pull in and out without backing onto a busy street. Every extension is poured with a minimum 5-inch 4,000+ PSI slab, rebar reinforcement, and a compacted gravel sub-base — specs that match or exceed your existing driveway.

We broom-finish the new concrete to match your existing driveway's texture as closely as possible. An expansion joint at the seam allows the old and new slabs to shift independently with Cypress's soil changes. Control joints are saw-cut at proper intervals to manage cracking in the new pour.

Most Cypress driveway extensions are completed in 1-2 days. We handle everything from the free estimate through site prep, forming, pouring, finishing, and cleanup. No minimum job size, no inflated pricing for small additions.

Driveway Extension FAQs — Cypress, TX

How much does a driveway extension cost in Cypress?

A single-car-wide driveway extension (10x20) typically costs between $2,800 and $4,800 depending on base preparation, access, and finish matching. Adding a second-car bay alongside an existing driveway runs $3,500 to $6,500. Every figure is an estimate — we provide a firm price at the free in-person estimate.

Can you match my existing Cypress driveway finish?

We do our best to match the existing color, texture, and broom finish of your current driveway. While perfect matching is impossible with concrete, we get close enough that the expansion joint becomes the only visible seam.

Do Cypress HOAs allow driveway extensions?

Cypress HOAs in communities like Bridgeland and Towne Lake have specific guidelines about hardscape additions. We can advise you on typical requirements during the estimate.

How long before I can park on a new driveway extension?

We recommend waiting 7 days before parking on the new slab. Full structural cure takes 28 days. Foot traffic is fine after 24-48 hours.
